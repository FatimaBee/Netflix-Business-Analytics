# 🎬 Netflix Business Analytics Platform

An end-to-end **Netflix Business Analytics Platform** combining Business Intelligence, Data Analytics, Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence to transform Netflix content data into meaningful business insights.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The Netflix Business Analytics Platform was developed to analyse Netflix's content catalogue and provide a combination of:

- 📊 Business Intelligence
- 🐍 Python-based Data Analysis
- 🧠 AI-powered Business Insights
- 🤖 Machine Learning
- 📈 Predictive Analytics
- 🌐 Flask Web Application

The project focuses not only on analysing historical content data but also on using data-driven approaches to support **future content planning and business decisions**.

---

# 🚀 Key Features

## 📊 1. Business Intelligence Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyse Netflix's content catalogue.

### Dashboard analysis includes:

- Total Titles
- Movies vs TV Shows
- Content Ratings
- Countries
- Genres
- Content Trends
- Release Years
- Content Distribution

# 🧠 3. AI Business Advisor

The AI Business Advisor analyses selected Netflix content characteristics and generates data-driven business insights.

It helps explore questions such as:

- Which types of content have stronger representation?
- How does content vary across genres?
- How does the catalogue differ by country?
- What patterns can be observed from historical content?
